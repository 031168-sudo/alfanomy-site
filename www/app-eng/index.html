    Alfanomy - GPS tracking in your pocket

    Alfanomy is a mobile application for owners of GPS trackers connected to the Alfanomy monitoring service.

    With Alfanomy you can keep an eye on your car, cargo or any other object from anywhere in the world, 24 hours a day.

    Main features:

    • Current location of your trackers on the map (GPS/GLONASS/LBS).

    • Device status: battery, GPS signal, satellites, last update time.

    • History of events for any period.

    • Track of movements on the map.

    • Sending commands to the tracker.

    • Push notifications about device events.

    To enter the application use the username and password you received when your tracker was connected to the Alfanomy service.

    Available for iPhone in the App Store and for Android in RuStore.
